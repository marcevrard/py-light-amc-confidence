# Instructions {-}

Each question has **four proposed answers** (A to D) and **one and only one**
correct solution. Two *implicit solutions* apply to **every** question, even
though they are not printed under it:

\input{template/implicit-answers.tex}

For **each** question, mark on the answer sheet one answer (A, B, C, D, n or all)
**and** one degree of confidence (DC). If you change your mind, use the **second
line** of that question: it replaces the first one. If you tick an answer but no
degree of confidence, **DC 3** is applied by default.
